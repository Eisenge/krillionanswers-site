Krillion Answers Today & Daily Dive Guide

Krillion is a free daily browser trivia game: seven prompts, about 25 seconds each, and rare answers score deeper than obvious ones. This guide explains the game, how to play and how to score better — the official game is at krillion.io.

What is Krillion?

Krillion, officially called The Daily Dive, is a daily browser game where you answer seven prompts and your score depends on how rare your answers are. The rarer the answer, the deeper you dive. It is free, plays in the browser and needs no account. Everyone gets the same seven prompts each day, so scores are comparable.

How the game works

  • Seven prompts — categories and open questions, from countries to dishes to songs.
  • About 25 seconds each — type one answer and confirm it before time runs out.
  • Rarity decides depth — uncommon valid answers sink your score; obvious ones barely count.
  • Official game — krillion.io.

Why people play it

The daily format brings fresh prompts every day, and the rarity scoring makes even a short game feel competitive. Because everyone answers the same prompts, sharing your depth score is part of the fun — and so is trying to beat yesterday's dive.

No. This is an independent, unofficial guide. The game belongs to its developer and is played at krillion.io; this site only explains it.
