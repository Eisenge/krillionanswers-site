ABOUT THE GAME / OVERVIEW

Krillion Game: rare-answer trivia in your browser

Krillion, officially called The Daily Dive, is a free daily browser trivia game where rare answers score higher than obvious ones. This page covers what it is, its origin and where to play.

What is the Krillion game?

Krillion is a daily browser game with seven prompts and about 25 seconds each. You type one answer per prompt, and the game scores how rare your answer is compared with other players. The rarer the answer, the deeper your score, described in the game as diving into the ocean.

Who made it

The current game at krillion.io was built by a solo developer who goes by the pseudonym kp. The name and idea trace back to One in a Krillion, a student game made at DigiPen (2024) that also appears on Steam. Krillion is an independent project, not a big-studio game.

Where to play

The official game is krillion.io. It runs free in the browser, no account or install required. The daily round uses the same seven prompts for every player, so scores are directly comparable.

Why people search for it

  • Daily freshness — new prompts every day keep players coming back.
  • Comparable scores — everyone answers the same prompts, which makes it shareable.
  • Simple rules — seven prompts, one answer each, rarity decides your depth.
  • Browser-only — nothing to download.

Frequently asked questions

  • Is Krillion free? — Yes, the daily game is free in the browser.
  • Is there an app? — Krillion is a browser game; the official experience is played at krillion.io.
  • Can I play more than once a day? — The daily round resets daily; krillion.io also has archive, themed packs and an unlimited mode.
  • Is this the official site? — No. This is an independent guide to the game.
