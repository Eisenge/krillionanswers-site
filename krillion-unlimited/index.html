Krillion Unlimited: play beyond the daily dive

The daily round gives you seven prompts. Krillion Unlimited removes that limit. This page explains the official unlimited mode and what community endless versions offer.

What is Krillion Unlimited?

Krillion Unlimited is a way to play Krillion without waiting for tomorrow's daily dive. Instead of seven fixed prompts, you keep answering category prompts one after another, scoring rarity the same way. It is useful for practice and for players who want more than one round a day.

The official unlimited mode

krillion.io itself offers an UNLIMITED mode (labelled with the infinity symbol) alongside archive and themed packs. Start the daily dive on the official site and look for the UNLIMITED entry. Because it is the official implementation, answers and scoring follow the same rules as the daily round.

Community endless versions

Several independent sites offer their own endless Krillion-style games, for example playdaily.org and alldle.net. These are third-party projects, not the official game, and their answer pools and scoring can differ. Play them at your own discretion; for the authentic experience use krillion.io.

Why players look for it

  • Practice without waiting — endless rounds mean more reps before the next daily dive.
  • Rarity habits — repeated play builds the habit of fast rare answers.
  • Beating the daily cap — one round a day can feel short once you are hooked.

Tips for endless rounds

The same rare-answer tips apply: prefer specific answers, think in subcategories and type fast. Endless mode is a low-stakes place to experiment with unusual answers.
