Privacy Policy

This privacy policy explains what data KrillionAnswers.online collects, how it is used, and the choices you have, in particular regarding Google Analytics and Google AdSense advertising.

Who operates this site

KrillionAnswers.online is an independent, unofficial fan guide to the browser game Krillion (The Daily Dive). We do not run the game and we do not ask you to create an account here.

Information we collect

We do not collect names, email addresses or account information. The site is static: your browser loads pages directly from our hosting provider. There is no login and no form that stores personal data.

Google Analytics

This site uses Google Analytics 4 to understand how many visitors we get and which pages are read. Analytics collects anonymous usage data such as pages visited, approximate region, device type and referring site. This data helps us keep the site useful and improve it.

Cookies

Google Analytics and Google AdSense may store cookies in your browser. These cookies help recognize returning visitors and serve relevant advertising. You can delete or block cookies in your browser settings at any time.

Advertising by Google AdSense

We may show ads served by Google AdSense. Google uses cookies (including the DoubleClick/DART cookie) to serve ads based on your visits to this and other websites. You can opt out of personalized advertising at adssettings.google.com; non-personalized ads may still be shown.

External links

Our pages link to krillion.io and other third-party sites. We are not responsible for their privacy practices; please read their own policies.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the site owner through the contact channel listed on this site.
